ABOUT US

Where student talent meets the digital future

The IT Skills Olympics is the University of Makati’s flagship inter-school competition, hosted by the College of Computing and Information Sciences. It gives students a stage to test their technical skills against peers from partner universities.

From programming and cybersecurity to networking and eSports, every category rewards creativity, discipline and teamwork.

EVENT INFORMATION

Everything you need to know

Read the guidelines before registering your delegates. Full rulebooks for each category are sent to registered schools.

Who can join

Currently enrolled students of any partner university. Each school may send one team or delegate per category, together with a faculty coach.

How to register

Send your school’s endorsement letter and the list of delegates to the organizing committee before the deadline. Slots are confirmed on a first-come, first-served basis.

Program schedule

The day opens with registration and orientation, followed by the opening ceremony, the category competitions and the awarding ceremony in the evening.

Rules and judging

Every category has its own rubric and time limit. The decisions of the panel of judges are final.

Venue and access

The competition is held on the University of Makati campus. Delegates should bring a valid school ID for entry.

Awards

Winners of each category receive medals and certificates, and the top-performing school is recognized as overall champion.
